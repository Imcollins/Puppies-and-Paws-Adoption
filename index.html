QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I inquire about a puppy?

Use the inquiry form below and include the puppy's name, your location and any questions you have. We’ll respond with the next steps.

Can I ask for more photos or videos?

Yes. Ask about the puppy you're interested in and request current photos or videos.

Do you provide documentation?

Available health, vaccination and other documentation can be discussed for each puppy before placement.

Do you offer delivery or pickup?

Available arrangements depend on the puppy and location. Contact us to discuss the options available for your situation.
